# Selected Projects

## Multi-Omics Disease Signature Discovery

Integrated metagenomic, metatranscriptomic, viral, functional, metabolomics-linked, and clinical cohort data to identify disease-associated molecular signatures, functional relationships, and biologically interpretable patterns across human cohorts. This work supports biomarker discovery, patient-relevant interpretation, and mechanism-focused translational biology.

## WAAFLE: Computational Method Development for Metagenomic Analysis

Co-developed WAAFLE, a computational method for profiling lateral gene transfer events directly from metagenomic data. This work involved problem formulation, algorithmic design, validation, biological interpretation, and communication of results, leading to a co-lead publication in <em>Nature Microbiology</em>.

## Clinical Biomarker Modeling for <em>Clostridioides difficile</em> Infection

Analyzed 10,000+ clinical fecal metagenomes across gastrointestinal disease cohorts and developed confounder-aware predictive models for disease-state classification. A first-author <em>Gut Microbes</em> study identified a 21-species predictive signature and log-odds scoring index for active <em>Clostridioides difficile</em> infection and post-FMT monitoring.

## Transkingdom Multi-Omics Analysis in MASLD

Contributed to multi-omic analyses of gut dysbiosis in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, integrating microbial, molecular, and clinical context to identify disease-associated patterns across biological kingdoms.

## Microbial and Metabolic Signatures in Microscopic Colitis

Contributed to analyses identifying distinct microbial and metabolic signatures associated with microscopic colitis, supporting disease-mechanism interpretation and translational biomarker discovery in human cohorts.

## Molecular and Epigenetic Mechanisms of Radiation-Induced Disease Biology

Led bench-based molecular and epigenetic studies of ionizing radiation-induced biological effects relevant to cancer risk, lung biology, carcinogenesis, fibrosis, transcriptional repression, inflammatory signaling, and disease mechanisms. This work contributed to 2 first-author and 5 co-authored peer-reviewed publications.
