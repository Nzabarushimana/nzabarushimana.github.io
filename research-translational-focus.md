# Research & Translational Focus

My work is focused on applying computational biology, bioinformatics, statistical modeling, and machine learning to complex biological datasets in ways that support translational research and therapeutic discovery. I have experience integrating NGS, metagenomic, metatranscriptomic, functional, viral, metabolomics-linked, and clinical cohort data to identify molecular signatures, biomarkers, disease mechanisms, and patient-relevant biological patterns.

## Multi-Omics Integration

I work across multiple data modalities, including metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, functional profiling, viral profiling, metabolomics-linked data, clinical cohort variables, and high-dimensional molecular features. My analyses emphasize quality control, confounder-aware modeling, robust interpretation, and biologically meaningful results.

## Biomarker Discovery and Patient Stratification

A central goal of my work is to identify molecular signatures and interpretable features that can distinguish disease states, support patient stratification, and generate testable biological hypotheses. This includes predictive modeling, feature selection, model evaluation, and translating model outputs into biologically interpretable findings.

## Disease Mechanisms and Translational Interpretation

I aim to connect computational results to mechanistic biology. My work spans host-microbiome interactions, inflammatory disease biology, metabolic disease biology, cancer-risk biology, epigenetic regulation, and molecular signatures of disease. I use literature, pathway-level interpretation, and biological context to move from statistical associations to the “so what” of disease biology.

## Reproducible Computational Biology

I develop reproducible workflows using Python, R, Bash, Linux/Unix, Git, workflow automation, high-performance computing, and documented analysis practices. My goal is to produce analyses that are scalable, interpretable, and reusable by collaborators and teams.

## Collaboration and Communication

I have worked closely with clinicians, epidemiologists, computational scientists, molecular biologists, and external collaborators to refine study design, troubleshoot complex datasets, interpret results, prepare manuscripts, present findings, and mentor junior scientists. I value clear scientific communication and iterative collaboration with experimental and clinical teams.
